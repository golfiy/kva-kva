Pro tips for standout streams

Get the most out of TopLive.

A few small things make a big difference — whether you're watching or sharing for the first time.

Set the mood with good lighting

Good light makes everything feel warmer and more alive — face the window, not your back to it.

Clear sound, clear vibe

Your voice is the main instrument — make sure it cuts through whatever's around you.

Talk to the room

Read names, react live — that's what separates streams from videos. The chat is the show.

Pick your topic and be found

Tag your stream so people who are already looking for what you do can actually find you.

What is TopLive?

TopLive is a social platform built around live video. Watch people share their hobbies and passions in real time, join the chat, or go live yourself — it's a community for both viewers and creators.

How do I watch a live stream?

Open TopLive, browse by topic or explore what's trending, and tap any stream to join. No account needed to watch.

Is TopLive free?

Yes — watching and going live are both free.

How do I find streams I'll like?

Browse by topic, follow people whose content you enjoy, or let TopLive suggest streams based on what you watch.

What devices are supported?

TopLive works on iOS, Android, and any modern desktop browser — your account follows you across all of them.

How is my privacy protected?

You control who sees you, who can message you, and what appears in your feed. Sensitive data is encrypted, and we never sell personal information to third parties.

How is the platform moderated?

A combination of active human moderators, automated systems for the obvious stuff, and clear community standards. Reports are reviewed within minutes during peak hours.

Can I go live too?

Absolutely. Going live takes seconds — just tap the Live button, choose your topic, and start sharing. No experience needed.
